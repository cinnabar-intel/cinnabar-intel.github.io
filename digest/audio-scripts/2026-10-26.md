Third, Infosys reported AI revenue up twenty-nine percent in a quarter. In the same release, it cut growth guidance to between one and two percent. AI revenue is not yet offsetting pressure on the core business.

Fourth, the Pragmatic Engineer studied twenty-seven companies. Monthly AI spend per developer ranged from one hundred fifty dollars to about three thousand. Few of them can measure the return.

Read together, these show one failure in four places. Disclosure, benchmarking, services pricing, and budget control were each designed for a slower curve. The winners will be the organizations that rebuild those processes, not the ones that simply spend more.
